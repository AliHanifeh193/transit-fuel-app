Fuel and trip expense management, in one place

Transit Fuel is designed for recording and reviewing fuel use, trip expenses, documents, reports, and company/driver approval and settlement workflows.

Service status: In development and testing. Public registration and live SMS sign-in are not yet available. This is an informational website and does not collect phone numbers or sign-in details.

Features

Fuel & trips

Record journeys, fuel quantities, consumption, and costs along routes and borders.

Reports & archive

Prepare reports and retain travel records and supporting documents for authorized users.

Financial workflow

Company and accounting review, approval, and settlement tracking by user role.

Secure access

Under testing: driver sign-in by one-time SMS code and two-factor sign-in for administrators and accounting staff. Not yet publicly available.

About Transit Fuel

Transit Fuel is independent of HeavyDriver. The first operational rollout is limited to Iranian phone numbers. Turkish SMS support is deferred.
