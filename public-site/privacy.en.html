Privacy policy — Transit Fuel prelaunch website

Current website

This prelaunch website has no registration, login or phone-number collection form. The pages do not include third-party advertising trackers or analytics. The hosting provider may retain technical access logs for security and delivery; hosting and retention details must be published before launch.

Future application

The future operational application may process account details and phone numbers, travel and fuel data, financial documents and audit events to deliver its services. One-time codes support authentication. Some drafts may be stored locally in the user's browser; readable backups must be protected.

Access and security

Drivers, company administrators and accounting staff have distinct access rights. Retention, correction/deletion requests and support contact details must be finalized before public launch.

Contact

The official contact channel has not yet been published. This page will be updated with verifiable support details before launch.
